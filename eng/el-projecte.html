A NEW LIFE FOR CAN COSTA

An exclusive space for art and
culture in the heart of Pollença

Welcome to a place where classical music, culture and history converge in an atmosphere of elegance and exclusivity. The birthplace of Miquel Costa i Llobera, one of Mallorca's most emblematic poets, has been restored and transformed into a private cultural centre, designed for those who appreciate artistic excellence and heritage preservation.


Can Costa's inaugural season took place in 2023 with a notable exhibition by artist Amador Magraner, which drew both residents and visitors from outside Mallorca. This first event marked the beginning of a new era for Can Costa as a leading cultural centre.
The Grand Hall, formerly a noble hall used for hosting events, now serves as a platform for promoting contemporary art and cultural activities, contributing to the enrichment of Pollença's cultural life.

The project's promoter, Joan Mateu Bou, born in Pollença, is a lawyer and philanthropist known for his contribution to preserving his community's cultural and historical heritage. Notable among his achievements is the upkeep of the Birthplace of Miquel Costa i Llobera, together with his family, located in the heart of the village of Pollença.
THE LEGACY OF THE BOU VILLALONGA FAMILY

Preserving the house and the figure of Miquel Costa i Llobera

Joan Bou Sunyerva was born in Felanitx in 1895 and became a prominent businessman who overcame the hardships of his time. While his six siblings emigrated to Chile out of necessity, he worked in Morocco before settling in Pollença, where he built a successful career. In 1931 he bought Costa's house from the poet's nephews for 80,000 pesetas, a significant investment at the time. He also acquired other important properties such as Son Brull and Alcanada. He devoted time and resources to preserving the house and Costa i Llobera's legacy, commissioning a portrait of the poet from Dionís Bennassar and a bust that can still be seen in the garden today.

His heirs, Maria Bou Villalonga and Joan Mateu Bou, have continued to preserve the house and promote its cultural legacy. Thanks to their commitment, the property has become a meeting place for cultural activities, keeping the memory of Costa i Llobera alive.

THE VALUES OF CAN COSTA

The Figure of the Poet and the Catalan Language

At Can Costa , our mission is to preserve the figure of the poet Miquel Costa i Llobera and spread his legacy beyond the borders of Mallorca. We strive to bring his work to the attention not only of Mallorcans or those already familiar with Catalan culture, but also to people from outside the island who may be less connected to it. At the same time, we work to promote the Catalan language, giving it visibility and recognition through our activities, and bringing the richness of his literary heritage to a diverse, international audience.

For 2025, various activities are planned to promote and create Catalan-language literature centred on the figure of Miquel Costa i Llobera . These activities will include literary events, talks, workshops and collaborations with authors and creators to enrich the knowledge and appreciation of his work.

The aim is to promote Catalan literature and bring Costa i Llobera's literary heritage to a broad and diverse audience, both locally and internationally, through republishing his work, digital promotion and tribute events.
