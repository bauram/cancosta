Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

At Can Costa we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information remains confidential. This privacy policy applies specifically to the use of our website's contact form.

Information Collection

When you use our contact form on the Can Costa website, we may collect personal information such as your name, email address and phone number. This information is provided voluntarily by you for the purpose of communicating with us and will be used solely for that purpose.

Use of Information

The information collected through the contact form will be used exclusively to respond to your queries, questions or comments. We will not use your personal information to send you unsolicited communications, nor will we share your data with third parties without your explicit consent. However, by sending us your message via email or through our form, you give your express consent for Can Costa to communicate with you by email, informing you about our events, promotions or other information.

Protection of Information

We are committed to protecting the security of your personal information. We use physical, electronic and administrative security measures to protect the data we collect through our contact form and to ensure it is used correctly.

Consent

By using our contact form, you agree to the collection and use of your personal information as set out in this privacy policy.

Access to and Correction of Personal Information

If you wish to access, correct or delete the personal information you have provided us through the contact form, please contact us using the details provided at the end of this privacy policy.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will take effect immediately upon publication on our website.

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy policy or the use of your personal information, please do not hesitate to contact us at the following address:
